Private by design

Your storage status
stays on your device.

DiskState reads storage capacity locally. It does not access file contents, photos, contacts, location, or an app-by-app storage history, and it sends no storage values to a developer-operated server. The main app may show advertising; the widget does not load an advertising SDK.
